Coach Bio
Ben van der Merwe

With over two decades of elite coaching experience since 2005, Ben van der Merwe brings an unmatched depth of sports science, strategy, and athletic development to the padel court. Having competed at the highest levels — including provincial hockey, SA top-league club rugby in Mpumalanga, and South African national chess — Ben understands the physical and mental blueprint required to win.

After mastering a diverse array of disciplines including rugby, hockey, javelin, and athletics, Ben transitioned his competitive drive and coaching expertise into padel two years ago. Now an active padel coach for the past year, he specialises in breaking down complex mechanics, sharpening tactical awareness, and building explosive court movement.

Whether you are a beginner looking to master the basics or an advanced player refining your match play, Coach Ben's multi-sport mastery will elevate your game at this boot camp.
